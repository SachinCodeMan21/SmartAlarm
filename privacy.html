Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the Smart Alarm application.

1. Data Collection and Storage

The Smart Alarm app is designed with privacy in mind. We do not upload your alarm settings, mission data, or personal information to any external servers. All data created within the app is stored locally on your device.

2. Permissions

To function correctly, the app requires specific permissions, including:

  • Notifications: To alert you when an alarm triggers.
  • Activity Recognition: Used for mission-related features.
  • Exact Alarm: To ensure your alarms trigger at the precise time requested.

These permissions are used ephemerally and processed only on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not share your data with third-party services or advertisers.
